How I work

Prices first, then a plan, then previews

I would rather show you the price than make you ask for it. So every block of work has a listed price, and you can add up your project before we ever talk.

  1. 01

    You see the price

    You stack the blocks your project needs in the calculator. A website starts at $1,450 and a mobile app at $3,800. The total you see is the total for that scope.

  2. 02

    We agree on a plan

    A 50% deposit, or payment in full, reserves your slot. On the kickoff call I turn your blocks into a plan and we set the delivery date.

  3. 03

    You watch it take shape

    You review progress on a private preview link at every milestone. Two revision rounds are included.

  4. 04

    I launch it and stay on

    I launch the project, hand over the keys and stay on for 30 days of support. If the scope changes along the way, the change is priced from the same blocks.

Who I work with

Individuals, small businesses and startups

Most of my projects are for three kinds of customer: people who want a personal site, small businesses that need to be found and booked, and startups building a first product. Each has a template in the builder and a page that itemizes the cost: personal website, small business website, SaaS website and mobile app MVP.

I also take on larger and enterprise work. Projects of that size, such as the Enterprise platform template at $32,605, go better with a conversation before any payment, so talk to me first.
