Sound and haptics test

Try each test on your phone and note which ones work. Turn the volume up first.

1. Your device

2. Chime

Plays the intro chime. On iPhone, website sound is muted while the ring/silent switch is on silent.

3. Chime, even in silent mode

Asks Safari to treat the chime like media playback, which plays through the silent switch. If test 2 is silent and this one is not, the silent switch is the reason.

4. Real switch

Flip this switch with your finger. If you feel a tick, your phone plays haptics for switches, which the site relies on for iPhone haptics. No tick usually means iOS is older than 18 or System Haptics is off (Settings, Sounds & Haptics).

5. The site's haptic

Tap this button. It uses the same method as every link and button on the site: an invisible switch under your finger. You should feel the same tick as test 4.
