What it is

What web development covers

Web development turns a design into a working site: pages that load fast on any screen, forms that reach you, content you can edit, and, when the project needs it, accounts, data and payments behind a login.

The builder separates the work into blocks so you only pay for the parts your project uses. A brochure site needs the foundation and its pages. A product adds authentication, a database and a billing block on top of the same foundation.

Questions

Web development questions

How much does web development cost?

A website starts at $1,450: the $1,200 foundation plus one page at $250. Each further page is $250, and each feature is a block with a listed price, for example $600 for a CMS or $700 for user authentication.

What does the foundation include?

The $1,200 website foundation includes: discovery call and project plan; responsive, mobile-first build; hosting setup and launch; two revision rounds; 30 days of post-launch support.

Can the site take payments or subscriptions?

Yes. Online payments cost $600 (secure one-time checkout powered by Stripe), subscriptions and billing cost $1,000, and a full online store costs $1,800.

What happens if I need something added later?

Changes after you pay are priced with the same blocks and the same prices you see in the builder. Adding a page or a feature later costs exactly what the price list says.

Do you take on larger or enterprise projects?

Yes, through a conversation first. Use the contact form or "Talk to me first" in the builder so the scope is agreed before anything is paid.
