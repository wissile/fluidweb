What it is

What SEO covers

SEO (search engine optimization) is the work that helps search engines find, understand and show your pages. Part of it lives in the code: page titles and descriptions, headings, structured data, a sitemap, clean addresses and fast loading. Part of it is deciding which pages to have and what they should say.

Here the SEO blocks are built into the site as it is made, so the work is done once and in the right place. They are one-time prices added to a website build. Nobody controls how a search engine ranks a page, so no ranking is promised.

Questions

SEO questions

How much does SEO cost?

SEO optimization costs $50 per page, added to a website build. Local SEO is $250, a keyword and content strategy is $400, and an SEO-safe migration with a 301 redirect map is $300.

Is SEO a monthly fee?

No. The SEO blocks are one-time prices that are part of the website build. If you want changes after launch, a 3-month care plan costs $300 and covers updates, backups, monitoring and two hours of changes each month.

Can you guarantee a first-page ranking?

No. Search engines decide rankings and nobody outside them controls the result. The SEO blocks cover the work that is in the site's control: meta tags, structured data, a sitemap, clean URLs and keyword-tuned headings.

I am replacing an old site. Will I lose my rankings?

The SEO-safe migration block ($300) is made for this: a 301 redirect map so you keep existing rankings when replacing an old site.
