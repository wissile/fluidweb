Cost

How much does a personal website cost?

A personal website costs $2,850 at Fluidweb: a four-page site with SEO optimization, a contact form, analytics and domain setup. The smallest site, one page on the website foundation, is $1,450.

Market

How this compares with published prices

$2,850 is inside the $800 to $10,000 range that Project Cost Estimator gives for a business website, and below its typical figure of about $4,000. The one-page starting price of $1,450 is inside its $300 to $3,000 range for a landing page. WebFX puts web design by a freelancer at roughly $500 to $10,000+. Published ranges cover many kinds of provider and scope, so read them as context. The itemized price above is what this exact set of blocks costs here.

Paying

What you pay and when

You can start with a 50% deposit of $1,425, with the balance invoiced before launch, or pay the $2,850 in full. Payments are processed by Stripe.

The builder estimates about 2 weeks for this build. That figure is a planning estimate, and the delivery date is confirmed on the kickoff call once your content and deadlines are known. Two revision rounds and 30 days of support after launch are included. The terms and the refund policy have the details.
