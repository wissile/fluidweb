What it is

What web design covers

Web design decides how each page looks and reads: the layout, the type, the colors, the images, and how all of it rearranges on a phone. A good design makes the next step obvious, whether that is reading on, booking a call or buying.

Design is a block in the builder like everything else. You pick one design option for the whole site and it is charged per page, so a small site stays small on the invoice. Brand work, imagery and a second color theme are separate blocks that you add only if you need them.

What is included

What you get

Every website starts on the $1,200 foundation, and each page is $250. That already covers:

  • Discovery call and project plan
  • Responsive, mobile-first build
  • Hosting setup and launch
  • Two revision rounds
  • 30 days of post-launch support

The page price includes a responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops. You see progress on a private preview link at each milestone.

Questions

Web design questions

How much does web design cost?

AI-designed, human-reviewed layouts are included in the $250 page price. Custom human design costs $120 per page and premium design with motion costs $220 per page. A brand identity kit is $600 and a design system is $900.

What is the difference between the three design options?

AI-designed, human-reviewed: layouts generated with AI, then refined and signed off by a designer. Custom human design: every page designed from scratch in Figma around your brand. Premium design + motion: custom design plus scroll animation, micro-interactions and art direction.

Do I get a logo and brand colors?

Only if you add the brand identity kit, which costs $600 and covers logo, color palette, typography and a one-page style guide.

How many rounds of changes are included?

Two revision rounds are included with every website. Extra rounds cost $300 each.
