Refund policy

The deposit is fully refundable until the kickoff call has taken place. After that, work already completed is not refundable and unused portions are refunded pro rata.

Last updated November 17, 2026

Before the kickoff call

If you change your mind before the kickoff call has taken place, you get back everything you paid, whether that was the 50% deposit or the full amount.

After the kickoff call

Once the kickoff call has taken place, work is under way. If you cancel after that point:

  • Work already completed is not refundable.
  • The portion of what you paid that covers work not yet done is refunded pro rata.

I will tell you which blocks are complete and which are not, and how the refund was worked out.

Care plans

Care plans are prepaid. If you cancel a care plan, the months not yet started are refunded pro rata.

How to ask for a refund

Send the request through the contact form, with the name and email address used at checkout. Refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe.

Governing law

This policy is part of the terms of service, which are governed by the laws of the State of Utah, USA.
