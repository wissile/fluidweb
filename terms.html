Terms of service

The agreement between you and Fluidweb when you order a website or a mobile app: what you get, what you pay and when.

Last updated November 17, 2026

Who these terms are between

Fluidweb is the freelance studio of Wissile Sogoyou, based in Salt Lake City, Utah, United States. In these terms "I" means Wissile Sogoyou and "you" means the person or business ordering a project. By paying for a build you agree to these terms.

What you are buying

A project is the set of blocks in your build at checkout: a foundation, a number of pages or screens, and the blocks you added. The price list describes each block. The website foundation includes: discovery call and project plan; responsive, mobile-first build; hosting setup and launch; two revision rounds; 30 days of post-launch support. The app foundation includes: discovery call and product plan; project setup and navigation shell; test builds on real devices; two revision rounds; 30 days of post-launch support.

The total shown in the builder is the price for that scope. Prices are in US dollars.

Payment

You choose at checkout to pay a 50% deposit or the full amount. The deposit reserves your slot and starts the project. If you pay the deposit, the balance is invoiced before launch and is due before the project goes live. Payments are processed by Stripe.

How the project runs

After payment we hold a kickoff call, where your blocks become a plan and a delivery date is set. You review progress on a private preview link at each milestone. The delivery estimate shown in the builder is a planning figure and not a commitment. The date agreed on the kickoff call depends on you providing content, access and feedback in good time.

Revisions

Two revision rounds are included with every website and every app. Further rounds cost $300 each for a website and $350 each for an app.

Changes to the scope

If you want to add or change something after you pay, the change is priced with the same blocks and the same prices as in the builder. I will confirm the price of a change with you before doing the work.

Support after launch

Every build includes 30 days of support after launch for bug fixes. Care plans extend this: for a website, $300 for 3 months or $1,000 for 12 months, and for an app, $600 for 3 months or $2,000 for 12 months. Care plans are prepaid and do not renew automatically.

What you provide

You are responsible for the content you give me, such as text, images and logos, and for having the right to use it. You also provide access to the accounts the project needs, such as your domain.

Ownership

Once the project is paid in full, the finished work made for you is yours. Third-party software, fonts, images and services used in the project stay under their own licenses. Unless you ask me not to, I may show the finished project in the Fluidweb portfolio.

Third-party services

Projects rely on services run by others, such as hosting, payment processing, app stores and email delivery. Those services have their own terms and prices, and I am not responsible for their outages or their decisions, including app store reviews.

Refunds and cancellation

The refund policy is part of these terms.

Warranty and liability

I build the project with reasonable skill and care and fix bugs reported during the support period. Beyond that the work is provided as it is, and I make no promise about search rankings, citations by AI assistants, traffic, sales or app store approval. To the extent the law allows, my total liability for a project is limited to the amount you paid for it.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Utah, USA.

Changes to these terms

I may update these terms. The version in place when you paid applies to your project.
