Privacy policy

What this site collects, why, and who handles it. In short: the details you type into a form, and analytics cookies only if you accept them.

Last updated November 17, 2026

Who is responsible

Fluidweb is the freelance studio of Wissile Sogoyou, based in Salt Lake City, Utah, United States. In this policy "I" means Wissile Sogoyou. To ask anything about your data, use the contact form.

What I collect

When you send a message

The contact form and the "Ask about this build" form collect your name, your email address, your company if you give it, your message, the topic you chose, your preferred call times if you give them, and the build you attached, if any. The message is sent to me by email. This site has no database and keeps no copy.

When you check out

The checkout form collects your name, your email address, your company and notes if you give them, the build you are ordering and whether you chose the deposit or full payment. Payment itself happens on a page run by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never pass through or get stored on this site.

When you browse

This site uses Google Analytics to see which pages are visited and how the builder is used. It is set up with Google Consent Mode, and analytics storage is switched off by default:

  • Until you choose, or if you decline, Google Analytics sets no analytics cookies. The Google tag still loads and may send limited, cookieless signals that are not tied to an identifier stored in your browser.
  • If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to tell visits apart and measure them.

Advertising storage and ad personalization stay switched off in both cases. This site runs no advertising. The builder sends a few events to Analytics, such as opening the checkout, copying a link to a build or sending a request, with the build's total, its products and its number of blocks. Those events never include your name, your email address or your message.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

What stays in your browser

Two things are saved in your browser's local storage and are not sent anywhere by being saved:

  • Your build. The builder saves the blocks you picked so they are still there when you come back. "Start over" deletes it.
  • Your cookie choice. Whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes both.

How I use it

I use what you send to answer you, to prepare a quote, to deliver the project you ordered, and to keep the records a business has to keep. I use analytics to improve the site. I do not sell your data and I do not use it for advertising.

Services that process data for this site

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any host, it processes technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver pages and protect the site.
  • Resend delivers the emails that the contact forms send to me.
  • Stripe processes payments and holds the payment details you enter on its page.
  • Google Analytics measures visits, as described above.
  • My email provider stores the messages I receive and my replies.

Each of these services handles data under its own terms and privacy policy, and some of them process data in the United States.

How long I keep it

I keep messages and project records for as long as I need them to answer you, deliver your project and meet tax and accounting obligations. Messages that do not lead to a project are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct it or ask me to delete it, through the contact form. I will do so unless I am required to keep a record, for example for accounting. Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights over your data.

Children

This site is meant for adults who want to commission a website or an app. It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts to collect something else or use a new service, I will update this page and the date at the top.
