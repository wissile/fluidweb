Cost

How much does a SaaS or startup website cost?

A SaaS website with a signed-in product costs $13,980 at Fluidweb: a ten-page marketing site plus user accounts, a database and API, a customer account area and subscription billing. A marketing site alone starts at $1,450.

Market

How this compares with published prices

$13,980 is inside the $5,000 to $120,000 range that Project Cost Estimator gives for a web app or SaaS. That range is wide because products differ so much in size, which is why this page lists every line. For the marketing pages alone, the same source gives $800 to $10,000 for a business website, and WebFX puts agency web design at roughly $3,000 to $30,000+. Published ranges cover many kinds of provider and scope, so read them as context. The itemized price above is what this exact set of blocks costs here.

Paying

What you pay and when

You can start with a 50% deposit of $6,990, with the balance invoiced before launch, or pay the $13,980 in full. Payments are processed by Stripe.

The builder estimates about 7 weeks for this build. That figure is a planning estimate, and the delivery date is confirmed on the kickoff call once your content and deadlines are known. Two revision rounds and 30 days of support after launch are included. The terms and the refund policy have the details.
