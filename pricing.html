Paying

Deposit and payment

You choose at checkout how to pay. On the Small business template ($7,730) the deposit is $3,865.

  • 50% deposit

    A 50% deposit reserves your slot and kicks off the project. The balance is invoiced before launch.

  • Pay in full

    One payment up front, with nothing left to settle at launch. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

  • Changes later

    Scope changes are priced with the same blocks and the same prices listed here, so adding a page or a feature later costs exactly what this page says.
