      FAQ

      Questions, answered

      How much does a website cost?

      A website starts at $1,450: the $1,200 website foundation plus one page at $250 per page. A site with 5 pages, AI-designed and human-reviewed layouts and no extra blocks costs $2,450. Custom human design adds $120 per page, and every other block shows its own price in the builder.

      How much does it cost to build a mobile app?

      A mobile app starts at $3,800: the $3,500 app foundation plus one screen at $300 per screen. An app with 8 screens and no extra blocks costs $5,900. That price covers one React Native codebase that ships to iOS and Android. Fully native iOS and Android apps add $4,500.

      What is included in the foundation price?

      The $1,200 website foundation includes: discovery call and project plan; responsive, mobile-first build; hosting setup and launch; two revision rounds; 30 days of post-launch support. The $3,500 app foundation includes: discovery call and product plan; project setup and navigation shell; test builds on real devices; two revision rounds; 30 days of post-launch support.

      How does block-by-block pricing work?

      Every project is priced as a foundation plus a price per page or screen, and each block you add has its own listed price. A block is either a one-time price or a price per page or screen, and the builder shows the math for every line. The total you see is the total you pay for that scope.

      How much do SEO, AEO and GEO cost?

      SEO optimization costs $50 per page and AEO / GEO optimization costs $50 per page, added to a website build. Local SEO is $250, a keyword and content strategy is $400, and an SEO-safe migration with a 301 redirect map is $300.

      What are AEO and GEO?

      AEO (answer engine optimization) and GEO (generative engine optimization) make your content easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI overviews to understand, quote and link to. The work covers answer-first content, crawlable server-rendered HTML, structured data and clear entity signals. It runs alongside classic SEO.

      How much do I pay up front?

      You pay a 50% deposit to reserve your slot, or the full amount if you prefer. With the deposit, the balance is invoiced before launch. On a $2,450 website the deposit is $1,225. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

      How long does it take to build a website or an app?

      The builder shows a delivery estimate that grows with the size of your build, starting at about 2 weeks. The Small business template ($7,730) is estimated at about 4 weeks. We confirm a delivery date on the kickoff call, once we know your content and deadlines.

      Can I get my project delivered faster?

      Yes. Rush delivery is priority scheduling that cuts the timeline by roughly 30%. It costs $1,000 for a website and $1,800 for a mobile app.

      What support do I get after launch?

      Every build includes 30 days of post-launch support with bug fixes. A 3-month care plan costs $300 for a website and $600 for an app, and a 12-month care plan costs $1,000 for a website and $2,000 for an app. The website plan covers updates, backups, monitoring and two hours of changes each month. Care plans are prepaid and do not renew automatically.

      How many revision rounds are included?

      Two revision rounds are included with every website and every app. Extra rounds cost $300 each for a website and $350 each for an app.

      Can I order a website and a mobile app together?

      Yes. Add both foundations in the builder and configure each one separately. They share one checkout and one project plan.

      What happens if I need to add something after I pay?

      Changes after you pay are priced with the same blocks and the same prices you see in the builder. Adding a page or a feature later costs exactly what the builder says.
