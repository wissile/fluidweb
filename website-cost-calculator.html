      How it adds up

      How the calculator works out the price

      Every total is a foundation, plus a price per page or screen, plus the blocks you switch on. A website is $1,200 plus $250 per page. A mobile app is $3,500 plus $300 per screen. A block is either a one-time price or a price per page or screen, and the summary shows the math for every line.

      The delivery estimate is a planning figure: about one week for every $2,000 of scope, with a minimum of two weeks. Rush delivery shortens it by roughly 30%.
