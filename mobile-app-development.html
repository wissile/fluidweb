What it is

What mobile app development covers

Mobile app development covers the design of each screen, the code that runs on the phone, the backend that stores accounts and data, and the release on the App Store and Google Play.

By default the app is a cross-platform React Native build: one codebase that ships to both stores, at no extra cost. Fully native iOS and Android apps, written separately in Swift and Kotlin, add $4,500. Screens that carry heavy logic, such as feeds, maps or editors, are $180 each on top of the $300 screen price.

Questions

Mobile app development questions

How much does it cost to build a mobile app?

A mobile app starts at $3,800: the $3,500 app foundation plus one screen at $300. An app with 8 screens and no extra blocks costs $5,900. The Mobile MVP template, with accounts, a backend, push notifications and store submission, is $12,990.

Is the app built for both iOS and Android?

Yes. The standard build is one React Native codebase that ships to both stores, included in the price. Fully native iOS and Android apps, written separately in Swift and Kotlin, add $4,500.

Do you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes, with the store submission block ($500): listings, review and release handled for both stores. A beta testing program on TestFlight and Play testing tracks is $200.

What support is there after launch?

Every app includes 30 days of post-launch support with bug fixes. A 3-month care plan costs $600 and a 12-month care plan costs $2,000. Care plans are prepaid and do not renew automatically.

Can I order an app and a website together?

Yes. Add both foundations in the builder and configure each one separately. They share one checkout and one project plan. A marketing landing page for the app is also available as a single block for $800.
