How much does a mobile app cost in 2026?

A mobile app costs roughly $10,000 to $50,000 when a freelancer builds it and $25,000 to $150,000 with an agency in 2026, according to Project Cost Estimator. At Fluidweb an app starts at $3,800, and a first release with accounts, a backend, push notifications and store submission is $12,990.

By Wissile Sogoyou·Last updated November 17, 2026

What do published price ranges say?

Project Cost Estimator gives two ranges, depending on who builds the app.

Both ranges are wide because an app can be a handful of simple screens or a product with accounts, payments, realtime features and offline use. The number of screens and what sits behind them decide the price.

What does a mobile app cost at Fluidweb?

A mobile app costs $3,800 at the least: the $3,500 app foundation plus one screen at $300. Each further screen is $300, so an app with 8 screens and no extra blocks is $5,900. That price covers one React Native codebase that ships to iOS and Android. The foundation includes: discovery call and product plan; project setup and navigation shell; test builds on real devices; two revision rounds; 30 days of post-launch support.

What makes an app cost more?

Screens with heavy logic, and everything that needs a server. These blocks account for most of the difference between a simple app and a product.

Should the app be cross-platform or fully native?

The standard build is cross-platform: a cross-platform React Native build that ships to both stores. It is included in the price. Fully native apps are separate Swift and Kotlin apps for maximum platform fidelity, and add $4,500. For a first version, one codebase keeps the cost down and gets the app onto both stores at once.

The Mobile MVP template is $12,990: ten custom-designed screens, three of them complex, with user accounts, a backend, push notifications, analytics, crash reporting, a beta program and store submission. The mobile app MVP cost page shows what moves that price up or down.

What do you pay to launch, and after?

App Store and Google Play submission is a block of its own at $500. A beta testing program is $200 and over-the-air updates, which ship fixes without waiting on a store review, are $200. Every app includes 30 days of support after launch for bug fixes. After that a care plan is optional: $600 for 3 months or $2,000 for 12 months, prepaid, with no automatic renewal.
